## 1 Scope

Applies to any evacuation of a production zone, whatever the cause: thermal, combustion,
chemical release or a security order. Personnel accounting is the same in every case.

## 2 When to evacuate

2.1 Evacuate immediately on any of:

- a machine at or above its critical pressure or temperature limit with people inside its
  15-metre perimeter (SOP-M04 §4.2.3);
- a declared fire in the zone (FIRE-EP-03 §2.2);
- an instruction from the plant security officer.

2.2 Evacuation **protects people; it does not remove the hazard.** An incident is never closed
because a zone was evacuated. The hazard's own remedy must still be carried out.

2.3 Evacuation is a high-risk action in production terms — it halts the line — so it requires
the system owner's authorisation.

## 3 Personnel accounting

3.1 The zone headcount is taken from the live worker tracking state, never from a static roster.
A stale roster is the classic cause of a missed person.

3.2 Report affected workers by **badge id and role**, so the shift supervisor knows who is
exposed and what they are qualified to do. "3 workers" is not an acceptable report on its own.

3.3 Workers marked OFF_SITE are excluded from the headcount even if their last known position was
inside the zone.

3.4 Muster at **Point Alpha** via the designated escape corridors. Confirm zero personnel by
thermal infrared sweep before reporting the zone clear.

## 4 PPE requirements

4.1 Mandatory PPE in ZONE_B and ZONE_C: ANSI-certified hard hat, high-visibility vest,
cut-resistant gloves, steel-toed boots.

4.2 A worker missing any mandatory item inside a hazardous zone is a compliance violation and
must be escorted out, whether or not an incident is in progress.

4.3 Maximum continuous shift in areas above 85 dBA: four hours without rotation.

## 5 Restricted and off-shift access

5.1 Presence in a restricted zone without an authorisation record is a security event, not only a
safety one — raise it to the plant security officer.

5.2 Presence outside the worker's rostered shift is treated the same way.

5.3 Dwell time beyond 20 minutes inside a zone with an active hazard is itself an alarm
condition, even if the worker is fully compliant on PPE.
